# Losercore Playlist

Unofficial fan project. Turn your photo into a PS2-style losercore video, and explore the story, tracks, and playlist behind the aesthetic.

- Website: https://losercoreplaylist.com
- Generator: https://losercoreplaylist.com/generator
- Playlist hub: https://losercoreplaylist.com/playlist
- What is losercore: https://losercoreplaylist.com/what-is-losercore

The free preview runs in your browser and does not upload your photo. This is a fan-made project, not affiliated with the official playlist.
